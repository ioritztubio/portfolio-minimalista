Direction: owner-pinned (Apple-like glass, monochrome, cinematic-elegant motion). No concept roll: brief-pinned direction beats the roll. Build path: code-led (no image generation available).

## Direction contract
THESIS: One continuous, living field behind a quiet monochrome interface; the page is a single flowing surface, not stacked horizontal bands. Refuses the dark-plus-neon developer portfolio and the name-as-hero template.
OWN-WORLD: Graphite/silver neutrals, light and dark. Colour exists only in a WebGL field of soft irregular shapes whose faint cool tint migrates per section. Frosted glass panels with bright rim highlights; liquid glass only on nav pill and portrait frame. Geist + Geist Mono for dates/tags.
STORY: Visitor learns what Ioritz builds and where he's heading, sees the shipped project, scans the path, leaves with the CV or a LinkedIn/email link.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: small name kicker, 3-line statement revealed word by word, a glass fact strip (Now / Studying / Based), CTAs View CV (solid ink), LinkedIn, Email. Right: portrait in liquid-glass frame (~1/3 width), tilts with pointer/gyro. Floating glass nav pill top centre (bottom tab bar on mobile).
FORM: Apple product-page cinematic scroll (pinned project stage, sticky-year timeline, full-screen closing statement). Seed: none; owner-pinned in their words: "I want movement, minimalism, transparency as apple, and I want it to be elegant and professional", "no orange or claudey style", and in the interview: monochrome, no accent, moving gradient field behind glass.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
